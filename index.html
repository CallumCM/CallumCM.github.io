Why the fire animation you ask?
In my chemistry class we recently were learning about entropy and I think a beautiful example of entropy is blackbody radiation. Blackbody radiation is the electromagnetic radiation that every atom always emits based on its temperature--it's the reason thermal cameras work and the reason molten metal glows white hot. It turns out you can calculate the blackbody spectrum using the Planck formula, so I got to work doing that. My website has no colors defined in it. I've defined every color as a temperature, and what you are seeing is the calculation of what the color of an object in real life would be at that temperature.
